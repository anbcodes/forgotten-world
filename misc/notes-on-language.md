# Some notes on the languages

Translation notes and other insights.

## The Elvish Tongue

This language seems to have primarly been spoken by the elves and some of the
kings of men. It shares some resemblance to our Latin, but we don't think they
are actually related.

The most striking similarity is the volews it uses for gender. Words ending in
"a" tend to be feminine (or common/neuter) and "o" tends to be masculine.

## The Dwarves

Very few people outside of the dwarves knew this language which made it fairly
hard to interpret. It also seems to have very little similarity to modern human
languages (at least any that we know).

## Men and Sumods

Men and sumods share a very similar language although different. They share a
lot of similarities with nordish or germanic languages almost enough that we
think they could be distantly related (although some is probably coincidence).

This is also the most common language and many elves and dwarves learned a
dialect of it (although this was more common it seems in the late period).

## Other Notes

We tried to preserve the original meaning in our translations, but this wasn't
always possible. At some point, we hope to provide some of the original texts
and our dictionaries and grammars, but it will take us time to compile them.

Also, this is a brief overview of the most common charateristics. All of the
above languages have significantly evolved over time and an elf in Urma in 200
EP would likely not understand an elf in Yolgo in 1000 LP. The same goes for
dwarves and men.
