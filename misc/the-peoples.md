# The Peoples

Found in an old book. Dated 500 LP.

The peoples of the world. There are four main peoples in this land, though not
frequently found together. Elves, dwarves, men and sumods. None get along all
that well with the other peoples, which is to be expected as they are indeed
very different.

*Of dwarves.* They are one of the oldest of all the peoples, some sources say
older than the elves. They have a lifespan of around 150 years and love the
earth. They primarily dwell in caves, preferring the light of torches over the
light of the sun. They have their disagreements with the other peoples but
mostly stay out of their way and mind their own business. They are excellent
craftsmen, especially with gold and silver.

*Of sumods.* One of the newer groups, these people seem to have come from men
but are slightly taller on average and speak rather quickly (at least according
to some of the elvish sources). They spend a lot of time building and discussing
but tend to disregard the other peoples. Some texts date their appearence to
around 1000 MP although others argue they existed much earlier but in such small
numbers as to not show up in most texts. Sumods tend to live around 100 years
and have some strange ideas and beliefs. They also are some the best inventors.

*Of elves.* Most proper of all groups and claim to be the oldest (dwarves
disagree). They primarily live in the woods and most men rarely see them. Their
natural lifespan is 1000 years and some have reached 2000. They keep the most
ancient written records and still more ancient stories are told in their songs
(which they are very fond of). Some look down on the other peoples for being
concerned with fleeting things, but others seek to teach others history and
understanding of the deep truths (as they call them).

*Of men.* Men are not nearly as old as the elves and dwarves but they are much
older than sumods. Most elvish sources date them to about 200 EP. Dwarves tend
to place them slightly later at 500 EP. Men tend to have lifespans slightly
shorter than sumods, about 80 years at the most. They tend to live everywhere
and everyone tends to stay out of their way except sumods, but they tend to get
along alright. Men are likely the least peaceful of all the peoples, frequently
going to war against one another for many reasons (most of which the elves do
not seem to understand), but are also the most welcoming to all and willingly
practice hospitality to strangers.

Much more could be written about each of these peoples and their are many more
minor groups throughout history, dragons, dryads, rulmoks, or even witchs,
furies, sidons, and the like. But this should suffice as a brief overview for
those who may be curious.
