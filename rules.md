# Rules (of the universe)

- Logic exists
- Supernatural exists (layers)
- Good exists (and evil)
- Multiple intelligent types of beings
  - Dragons (strong, powerful, good and evil, greedy)
  - Elves (high beings, know nature)
  - Dwarves (know non-living nature)
  - Men (ancient men)
  - Somod (modern western man, utilitarian, ignores other creatures)
  - Dryads and nyads

Goal: take modern philosophy and ideals and put them into ancient myths
