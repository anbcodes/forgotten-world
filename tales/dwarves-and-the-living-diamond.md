# The dwarves and the living diamond

Dated 200 LP.

The following is an account of Dwain's search for the living diamond which
according to all tales and records I've received, occured around year 500 MP.

Dwain was one of the five mighty kings of the dwarves. He ruled under the south
western kingdom called Ergad. Ergad is one of the ancient kingdoms of the dwaves
and to this day none have mapped the deepest of their mines. Some tunnels have
likely not been visited since the early period. The dwarves of Ergad were proud clearly taking from the name of their city
